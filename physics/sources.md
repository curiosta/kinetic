# Sources: Physics in five minutes

Every number on this page is either a figure already on a Kinetic sport page (named in the chapter) or derived by the arithmetic shown, from the constants below.

## Constants and textbooks

- [NIST: standard acceleration of gravity](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?gn): g = 9.80665 m/s², used as 9.81
- [NIST: standard atmosphere](https://physics.nist.gov/cgi-bin/cuu/Value?stdatm): sea-level pressure 101.325 kPa
- [NIST SP 811: guide for the use of the SI](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/pdf/sp811.pdf): newton, pascal, joule, bar; 1 kcal = 4,184 J
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1: Newton's second law](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/5-3-newtons-second-law): F = m a, weight = m g
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1: work](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/7-1-work): work = force × distance, m g h
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1: fluids, density and pressure](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-1-fluids-density-and-pressure): pressure from the weight of a fluid column
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1: Archimedes' principle and buoyancy](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/14-4-archimedes-principle-and-buoyancy): buoyant force = weight of displaced fluid
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 2: molecular model of an ideal gas](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-2/pages/2-1-molecular-model-of-an-ideal-gas): gas pressure and volume (Boyle's law)
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1: friction](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/6-2-friction): friction force = μ × normal force
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1: centripetal force](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/6-3-centripetal-force): m v² ÷ r, banked and leaning turns
- [OpenStax University Physics Vol. 1: impulse and collisions](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/9-2-impulse-and-collisions): force × time = change in momentum
- [NASA Glenn: the drag equation](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/drageq.html): D = ½ ρ v² Cd A
- [NASA Glenn: Earth atmosphere model](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/atmosmet.html): pressure falls with altitude (standard atmosphere)
- [NASA Glenn: Boyle's law](https://www.grc.nasa.gov/www/k-12/airplane/aboyle.html): P × V constant at fixed temperature

## Notes

- g = 9.81 m/s² (NIST standard value 9.80665). Sea-level pressure 101.325 kPa. Seawater 1,025 kg/m³, fresh water 1,000 kg/m³, 1 kcal = 4,184 J.
- Illustrative inputs (20% muscle efficiency, μ values, CdA values, 6 L lung volume) are the ones the sport pages use, and are labelled as illustrative on the page.
