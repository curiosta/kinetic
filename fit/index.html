01 · Your body in the physics

Engine, shock absorber, life support

Every sport page on Kinetic works out the forces. Three of them land on you.

ENGINE

You make the power

Lifting yourself is work: mass × g × height. A 70 kg climber gaining 1,000 m does about 687 kJ of lifting work; muscles turn only about a fifth of food energy into that, so it costs roughly 820 kcal.

From our mountaineering page (illustrative).

SHOCK ABSORBER

Your legs set the force

Drop from 0.5 m and you land at about 3.1 m/s. Stopping in 5 cm with stiff legs, the ground pushes back with about 11× your weight on average. Bend your knees to stop over 25 cm and it falls to about 3×.

Average force = weight × (1 + drop ÷ stopping distance). Illustrative.

LIFE SUPPORT

Heart and lungs keep up

At 5,029 m on the HMI basic-course route, air pressure is about 53.8 kPa, so the oxygen pressure in each breath is about 53% of sea level. Underwater, your lungs handle changing pressure instead.

Standard atmosphere, from our mountaineering and scuba pages.

A stronger engine leaves more in reserve, trained legs absorb more, and a fitter heart and lungs have more margin. That margin is what the next five sections build.

02 · The baseline

The baseline, and a 6-week start with no gym

WHO's adult target: 150–300 minutes of moderate activity or 75–150 minutes of vigorous activity a week, plus muscle-strengthening on 2 or more days. WHO 2020 Fit India protocol ICMR-NIN gives the same shape for Indian adults: 30–60 minutes of moderate activity on at least 5 days, and strength work on at least 2. ICMR-NIN 2024

How hard is moderate? You can talk but not sing. If you can't say more than a few words without pausing for breath, that's vigorous. CDC

From week 5, walk on the strength days too.

Progression rule. Add 5–10 minutes to each session every 1–2 weeks over the first 4–6 weeks. After that, add days or intensity as you tolerate it, and step back if you don't. Dept of Health HK

Strength, no equipment

Squats, lunges, push-ups (on the knees or against a wall to start), plank, partial curl-ups, stairs. Stop a set when your form starts to slip. Fit India protocol ICMR-NIN 2024

Balance and breath

Vrikshasana on each leg, walking backwards, yoga asanas and pranayama. Fit India protocol ICMR-NIN 2024

Rest is part of it. Shortness of breath, pain, nausea, vomiting or headache are signs your body needs rest. ICMR-NIN 2024 Check yourself at week 0 and week 6 with the Fit India tests: 2 km walk or run, push-ups, 30-second partial curl-ups, sit-and-reach and tree pose. Fit India protocol

04 · Food, water, sleep

Eat, drink and sleep for the training

General guidance for healthy adults from India's dietary guidelines. ICMR-NIN 2024

  • Protein from food. Cereals and pulses together (about 3 parts to 1), with milk, eggs or meat if you eat them. ICMR-NIN advises against protein supplements; most athletes can get enough protein from food.
  • Plenty of vegetables, at least 400 g a day, and no more than 5 g of added salt.
  • Water. About eight glasses (around 2 litres) a day for a healthy adult, more in heat and with hard activity. Buttermilk, tender coconut water or lemon water help in hot weather; soft drinks don't replace water.
  • Heat. Drink water even if you're not thirsty, train in the cooler morning or evening, and avoid strenuous activity outdoors in the afternoon. MoHFW heat advisory
  • Sleep 6–8 hours.

Safety No alcohol before diving or flying

Alcohol slows reaction time and judgement and adds to dehydration, a risk factor for decompression sickness. DAN: "alcohol and diving are not compatible." DAN Indian rules say the same for flying: Mizoram's aero-sports rules turn away anyone who has had alcohol in the 8 hours before an operation, Mizoram Aero Sports Rules 2021 and Gujarat's guidelines bar anyone under the influence from aero and water sports. Gujarat Tourism adventure guidelines For flying after diving, see the scuba page.

05 · Doctor first

When to see a doctor before you start

If you're healthy with no symptoms, you can usually begin light-to-moderate exercise without medical clearance and build up gradually. ACSM 2015 Check with a doctor first if any of these apply:

  • Chest pain or pressure, breathlessness at rest or with light effort, fainting, dizziness, or palpitations. ACSM 2015
  • A heart, lung, kidney or blood-pressure condition, asthma or diabetes. ICMR-NIN 2024 ACSM 2015
  • Seizures, blackouts or a head injury. Diver Medical Questionnaire BHPA
  • Surgery, a fracture or a hospital stay in the past year. Diver Medical Questionnaire Ministry of Tourism guidelines
  • Pregnancy, or trying to conceive (and no diving). Diver Medical Questionnaire
  • Regular prescription medicine. Diver Medical Questionnaire
  • Ear, sinus or eye problems, if you plan to dive. Diver Medical Questionnaire
  • Over 40 and planning vigorous training; the diving form also asks everyone over 45 for more detail. ICMR-NIN 2024 Diver Medical Questionnaire

Schools aren't doctors; PADI says so plainly. PADI Fill in the operator's own medical form honestly, and if you feel ill on the day, skip it. Diver Medical Questionnaire

A self-check, not a medical clearance or certification.

01 · HealthIn the past year, have you had chest pain or pressure, breathlessness at rest or with light effort, fainting or dizziness, or a racing or irregular heartbeat?

These are the warning signs that call for medical clearance before starting exercise. ACSM 2015 Diver Medical Questionnaire

02 · HealthHas a doctor told you that you have a heart, lung (including asthma), kidney or blood-pressure condition, or diabetes?

ICMR-NIN asks people with these conditions to consult a physician before taking up an exercise programme. ICMR-NIN 2024 ACSM 2015 BHPA

03 · HealthHave you ever had a seizure, a blackout, or a head injury with loss of consciousness?

Both the diving and the paragliding forms ask about this. Diver Medical Questionnaire BHPA

04 · HealthHave you had surgery, a fracture or a hospital stay in the last 12 months?

On the diving form and on India's bungee medical restrictions. Diver Medical Questionnaire Ministry of Tourism guidelines

05 · HealthAre you pregnant, or trying to become pregnant?

The diving form says not to dive; India's bungee guidelines list pregnancy as a restriction. Diver Medical Questionnaire Ministry of Tourism guidelines

06 · HealthDo you take prescription medicine regularly (other than birth control)?

The diving form asks for a doctor's sign-off for most prescription medicines. Diver Medical Questionnaire

07 · Your baselineIn a usual week, how much brisk activity do you do, the kind where you can talk but not sing?

WHO's adult minimum is 150 minutes of moderate activity a week. The talk test is CDC's rule of thumb for moderate. WHO 2020 CDC

08 · Your baselineDo you do muscle-strengthening work (squats, push-ups, carrying loads) on 2 or more days a week?

WHO and ICMR-NIN both ask for at least 2 days a week. WHO 2020 ICMR-NIN 2024

09 · Your baselineCan you walk 1.6 km in 14 minutes without stopping?

Here a No means build up first, whatever your sport. If you plan to dive, the diving medical form sends anyone who can't do it to a doctor first. Diver Medical Questionnaire

10 · Your baselineCan you hold Vrikshasana (tree pose) for 10 seconds on each leg?

Fit India's balance test: 10 seconds, or three breaths, on each side is the target. Fit India protocol

11 · Water sportsCan you swim 200 m without stopping and float or tread water for 10 minutes, with no aids?

PADI's swim test for Open Water. Surf and kite schools ask for less, but always for some. PADI Kallialay Quest

12 · RecoveryDo you usually sleep 6 to 8 hours?

ICMR-NIN's guidance for healthy sleep. ICMR-NIN 2024
