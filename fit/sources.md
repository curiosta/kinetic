# Get fit (/fit/): sources (checked 23 Oct 2026)

General guidance only. No prices, no supplements, no medical diet plans.

| Key | Source | Used for | How checked |
|---|---|---|---|
| WHO 2020 | WHO Guidelines on physical activity and sedentary behaviour, https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015128 | 150–300 min moderate or 75–150 min vigorous a week; strength on 2+ days | Figures as quoted in the Fit India protocol (WHO pages timed out from the fetcher) |
| Fit India | Fitness Protocols for Age 18–65, MoYAS, https://yas.nic.in/sites/default/files/Fitness%20Protocols%20for%20Age%2018-65%20Years%20v1%20(English).pdf | Test battery (2 km walk/run, push-ups, partial curl-ups, sit-and-reach, Vrikshasana), no-equipment moves | Full PDF text read (mirror copy) |
| ICMR-NIN 2024 | Dietary Guidelines for Indians 2024, https://nin.res.in/dietaryguidelines/pdfjs/locale/DGI_2024.pdf | 30–60 min on 5+ days; strength 2+ days; warning signs; physician first with heart disease, BP, diabetes, asthma etc.; over-40 before vigorous; protein from food, no supplements; cereal:pulse ~3:1; ≥400 g vegetables; ≤5 g salt; ~8 glasses water; 6–8 h sleep | Full 148-page PDF text read (Guideline 10, pp. 69–71, plus diet sections) |
| CDC | Measuring physical activity intensity, https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/measuring/ | Talk test | Page read |
| Dept of Health HK | CHP Doctors' Handbook ch. 4, https://www.chp.gov.hk/archive/epp/files/DoctorsHanbook_ch4.pdf | Progression: add 5–10 min every 1–2 weeks over the first 4–6 weeks (after ACSM) | PDF read, Fig 4.1 |
| ACSM 2015 | Riebe et al., Updating ACSM's recommendations for exercise preparticipation health screening, https://journals.lww.com/acsm-msse/Fulltext/2015/11000/Updating_ACSM_s_Recommendations_for_Exercise.28.aspx | Healthy, no-symptom people can start light–moderate without clearance; symptoms/known disease → clearance | Article read |

| PADI OWD | https://www.padi.com/courses/open-water-diver | 200 m swim (or 300 m snorkel), 10 min float/tread | Page read |
