# Paramotoring: sources and verification log

Checked 3 Oct 2026 (IST). Every fact on the page comes from a source listed here or is standard physics worked on the page. Course fees, phone numbers and email addresses were deliberately **not reproduced**. No licence rules were invented: the page reports what each source says and attributes it.

## 1. Physics (worked on the page)

| Item | Basis | Status |

## 2. Rules and guidance (India)

| Item | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|
