04 · About the data

How we built this calendar

Peak means a cited source calls those months the best, peak or ideal time, or every source we cite agrees on the season. Where one source gives a narrower peak, the narrower one wins. Possible means the activity runs but it's a shoulder or part month, it depends on the weather, or our sources disagree. Off means closed, banned, or outside every season we found. Year-round rows, like indoor rinks, pools and ranges, say so.

When sources disagree, we show the more cautious option and say so in the month's note. Monsoon timings come from the India Meteorological Department's normal onset and withdrawal dates. A normal date is an average, so any given year can run early or late.

Events show an exact date when we have a verified race day (editions that are already over move to “Already held”, newest first). Recurring events whose next dates are not announced yet sit under Coming up in their usual month, marked “Dates not announced yet”, with the last known edition where we have it. We do not invent dates. Events we could not verify as recurring are listed in the sources file.

Every row, event and dropped item is logged with its source in sources.md. Seasons and events were checked on 5 Oct 2026. Each sport's own page has more detail under “When to go”.
