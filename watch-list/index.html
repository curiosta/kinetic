Water

Cave diving

At 30 m every breath takes about four times the gas it does at the surface, and with no straight swim up, cave divers turn back after using a third of their gas.

India
No Indian school or governing body found as of Oct 2026. India has recreational and technical dive centres, but we found no cavern or cave course.
World body
CMAS, the World Underwater Federation
Learn abroad
  • Krabi Scuba Club, IANTD cavern to technical cave, Krabi, Thailand
  • Tech Dive Thailand, cavern and cave courses at Song Hong and Spider Cave, Thailand
Prerequisites
Minimum age 18, 15 with parental consent; Provide proof of TDI Cavern Diver certification or equivalent (TDI Intro to Cave Diver)

Water

Ice diving

Fresh water is densest near 4 °C, so the coldest water sits right under the ice, and expanding gas can chill a regulator until it freezes open.

India
No Indian school or governing body found as of Oct 2026. We found no ice-diving course or operator in Ladakh, Himachal Pradesh or Kashmir.
World body
CMAS, the World Underwater Federation
Learn abroad
  • Shiretoko Diving Project, drift-ice dives, Rausu, Hokkaido, Japan
Prerequisites
CMAS 2* Diver or equivalent; 50 logged open water dives in total, at least 20 must be after the 2* diver certification (CMAS Ice Diver)

Water

Big-wave surfing

A wave's energy grows with the square of its height, so a 15 m face carries about 25 times the energy of a 3 m one.

India
No Indian school or governing body found as of Oct 2026. India's surfing federation covers shortboard, longboard and stand-up paddle.
World body
International Surfing Association (ISA)
Learn abroad
  • Jet Resgate, tow-in surf and big-wave rescue course, Nazaré, Portugal

Snow & ice

Snowkiting

The kite pulls in the moving air while your edges grip the snow, and like a sail and a keel the two forces drive you across the wind and even upwind.

India
No Indian school or governing body found as of Oct 2026. Gulmarg's ski schools teach skiing and snowboarding; we found no snowkite lessons.
World body
International Kiteboarding Association (IKA)
Learn abroad
  • Haugastøl kite courses, weekend snowkite courses on the Hardangervidda plateau, Norway
  • Swiss Kitesurf snowkite school, Lake Silvaplana and the Bernina Pass, Switzerland

02 · How we checked

How we checked

For each sport we searched the national federations recognised by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, the Indian federation of the sport's world body, and Indian schools and operators. A sport goes on this list only if we found none of them. Sports where we did find an Indian school, operator or body are left off, even if you still have to go abroad to compete.

Not finding one doesn't prove there isn't one. If you know an Indian school or body for any sport here, tell us below and we'll check it.

Prerequisites are quoted from the certifying body's own document and linked. Where we could not find one, we leave it out. Learn-abroad links go to the school's or federation's own site. Checked 19 Oct 2026.
